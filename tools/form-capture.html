Form Capture

A one-tap tool that lists the fields on your company's mileage page, so Companion can be set up to fill that form for you later.

What it does, and doesn't do

It lists
  • Field labels, names and types (for example "Date", "Odometer start", number box)
  • Drop-down choices and button names (for example "Add trip", "Submit")
  • The page's site name and path
It never
  • Reads anything typed into the form, including your password
  • Sends anything anywhere. It only shows a report on your screen.
  • Clicks, changes or submits anything on the page

Long numbers and email addresses in labels or lists are hidden with # and [email]. You see the whole report before copying it. Check with IT before using tools on company systems.

On a computer (Edge or Chrome)

  1. Show the favorites bar: press Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag this button onto the favorites bar: Capture form
  3. Sign in to the mileage portal and open the page where you enter mileage.
  4. Click Capture form on the favorites bar. A report appears on top of the page.
  5. Tap Copy report and paste it to Claude.

On an Android phone (Chrome)

  2. Bookmark this page (⋮ → ☆). Then open ⋮ → Bookmarks, press and hold the new bookmark, and tap Edit.
  3. Change the name to Capture form. Clear the URL box and paste. Save.
  4. Sign in to the mileage portal and open the mileage entry page.
  5. Tap the address bar, type Capture form, and tap the bookmark that appears (the one with the star, not a search).
  6. Tap Copy report and paste it to Claude.

If the report says a section is "blocked", that part of the page comes from another site. Open that part on its own (often a "pop out" or "open in new tab" option) and run Capture form again there.
