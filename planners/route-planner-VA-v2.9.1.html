How to get your calls from SAP
  1. In SAP, type IW49N in the command field and press Enter.
  2. Select Get Variant (or press Shift+F5).
  3. Choose your service-call variant and confirm.
  4. Execute the report (clock icon, or F8).
  5. In the results, click the Select All icon in the upper-left corner of the list, so every row is highlighted.
  6. Copy with Ctrl+C (or right-click > Copy Text).
  7. Click in the box above, paste with Ctrl+V, and select Load calls.
