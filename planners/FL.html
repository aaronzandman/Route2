      Usage data
      
To see how many people use the planner, on what devices and in which areas, the planner sends a small anonymous count when you open it on a phone or tablet (once a day) and when you use certain features there. Nothing is sent when you use it on a computer. It's always on, and everything it sends is listed here.

      
What's sent
    
        
  • Which planner and version (on this device: )
    • 
        
  • What you used, by name only: opened, loaded calls, opened a route, sent to phone, sent feedback, took the tour
    • 
        
  • Your anonymous ID (below), once a day, so you're counted as one person across days and devices
    • 
        
  • Screen size, plus browser and device type (read from your browser)
    • 
        
  • General area (country and state), worked out by the counting service from your internet address. The address itself isn't stored.
    • 
      

      
What's never sent
    
        
  • Your calls, order numbers, descriptions, or serial numbers
    • 
        
  • Store numbers, addresses, or your GPS location
    • 
        
  • Your name, P number, email, or anything you type
    • 
        
  • Cookies, or any ID that identifies you or this device
    • 
        
  • The page address (it can hold call data or your Windows user name), so it's blocked from every count
    • 
      

      
Where it goes
Counts go to GoatCounter, a privacy-focused counting service. Like any website, it sees your internet address while receiving a count; it uses it only to work out country and state and to avoid double counting for a few hours, and never saves it. No personally identifiable information is collected. Only the app's owner sees the totals, and only as counts (for example "42 people opened the Virginia planner this week"). Counts are only sent when you're online.

      How to get your calls from SAP
      
    
        
      
          
    1. In SAP, type IW49N in the command field and press Enter.
      2. 
          
    2. Select Get Variant (or press Shift+F5).
      3. 
          
    3. Choose your service-call variant and confirm.
      4. 
          
    4. Execute the report (clock icon, or F8).
      5. 
          
    5. In the results, click the Select All icon in the upper-left corner of the list, so every row is highlighted.
      6. 
          
    6. Copy with Ctrl+C (or right-click > Copy Text).
      7. 
          
    7. Click in the box above, paste with Ctrl+V, and select Load calls.
